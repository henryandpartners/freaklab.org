Hologram festival

Hologram Festival is the festival of holographic arts, media, and technology hosted by the futuristic research group at KMUTT.

Bangkhuntien

About the Festival

Hologram Festival brings together artists, designers, scientists, engineers, and other professions to create enchanted ensembles redesigning everyday environments through holographic projection technology. The goals of the festival is to explore new methods of storytelling through innovative medium, and promotes suspension of disbelief as the way to redefine possibilities. In order to unravel the complex challenges facing our society today, one must have a great imagination to think beyond impediments and mundane reality. With the hologram, we believe that it is not out of reach to empower people to have a moment of “Cognitive Estrangement” that would allow them to be the agent of changes.

The date of the festival is TBD.

Freak Lab

Futuristic Research in Enigmatic + Aesthetics Knowledge (Freak) Lab is an anti-disciplinary research group that focus on exploring futures by using design, prototype, and research as tools to reimagine reality. The community of Freak fuses open-minded peoples from various disciplines, ages, and expertise to work together on cutting edges projects. Freak lab functions as the hub for transforming futuristic ideas into practical prototypes that have high impact to society (and vice versa) in term of economy, epistemology, philosophy, and aesthetics.
